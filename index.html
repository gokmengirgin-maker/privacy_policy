Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use any of Our mobile applications ("the Applications" or "the App").

1. Data Collection & Offline Preferences

We value your privacy. Our applications are designed with privacy-first principles. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal identifying information (such as your name, personal email address, contact list, or precise real-time GPS location) to third parties.

Depending on the specific features of the Application you use, local data such as bookmarks, offline notes, settings, high scores, or game progress are saved directly on your device's local storage.

2. Anonymous Accounts & Online Features

Some of Our applications may offer online features (such as high score leaderboards, cloud sync, or user preferences). For these features, We may utilize secure third-party services (such as Google Firebase) to generate an anonymous user identifier. This identifier allows you to participate in global rankings and sync data without requiring personal identity registration.

3. Third-Party Services & Advertising

Our applications may integrate third-party services that collect non-personal information to provide core functionality, analytics, and advertisements. These third-party providers have their own independent privacy policies:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to display advertisements (banner, interstitial, or rewarded ads). AdMob may use device advertising identifiers (e.g., Android Advertising ID) to serve non-personalized or personalized ads and monitor ad performance. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Firebase: Used for secure anonymous user authentication, cloud databases (Firestore), and crash reporting. Learn more at Firebase Privacy Policy.

4. Children's Privacy

Our applications do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately delete such information.

5. Security of Your Personal Data

The security of Your data is important to Us. We use standard administrative and technological safeguards (including encrypted HTTPS/SSL connections) to protect any data transmitted through Our Applications.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update Our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our official support channels.
