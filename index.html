Privacy Policy for Holy Bible Offline

Last updated: July 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes Our policies and procedures on the collection, use, and disclosure of Your information when You use the Holy Bible Offline application ("the App").

1. Offline-First & Personal Data Collection

We respect your privacy. The App is designed as an offline-first Scripture reading application. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal identity, email address, contacts, or location data to any external server owned by us.

Your bookmarks, notes, and highlights are stored locally on your device in a secure local database.

2. Advertising & Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

The App uses Google AdMob to serve non-intrusive advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and use pseudonymous device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) to serve relevant, respectful ads.

  • Content Rating: We enforce General Audience (G-rating) content filters for all advertisements displayed.
  • For more information on how Google handles advertising data, visit Google's Privacy & Terms Policy.

3. Permissions

  • Notifications: Used solely to schedule local daily verse reminders on your device. Notifications operate completely locally without external servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our application does not address anyone under the age of 13 in a harmful manner. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
