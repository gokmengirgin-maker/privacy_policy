This Privacy Policy describes the policies and procedures of Gokmen Girgin ("Developer", "we", "us", or "our") regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use Solitaire Deluxe: Card Games ("the Application").

1. Information We Collect and Receive

Solitaire Deluxe is designed as an offline-first card game. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, or physical location:

  • Local Data Storage: Game progress, high scores, coins, unlocked card decks, table themes, and audio preferences are stored exclusively on your device using local storage.
  • Custom Gallery Photos: If you choose to personalize card tables or card backs using images from your device gallery, the chosen photo remains strictly on your device and is never uploaded externally.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

The Application integrates third-party services that process non-personal diagnostic data and Advertising IDs to display in-game ads:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): Used to serve banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. Learn more at Google Privacy & Terms.

3. Permissions

  • Photos / Storage: Used only when you choose to set a custom table or card back image from your gallery.
  • Internet Access: Used solely to load advertisements from Google AdMob.

4. Children's Privacy

Solitaire Deluxe does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. The game contains family-friendly content suitable for all ages.
